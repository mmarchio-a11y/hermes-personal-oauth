PERSONAL-USE GOOGLE INTEGRATION

Italo Hermes Agent

Italo is a personally operated AI assistant built using Hermes Agent. This Google integration is used by its owner to work with their own email, calendar, files, documents, contacts and tasks. It is not a public sign-up service.

What the integration does

With the owner’s authorization, the assistant can retrieve information, summarize email and documents, prepare work, and carry out permitted actions in Google services. Email sending, sharing and other changes are governed by the owner’s instructions and configured approval rules.

Google access

The configured permissions cover Gmail reading, sending and modification; Calendar; Drive; Contacts; Sheets; Docs; Slides; and Tasks. OAuth consent grants technical access, not blanket permission for the assistant to take every action.

Privacy and contact

Google data is not published on this website. Read the privacy policy for details about processing, storage and revoking access.

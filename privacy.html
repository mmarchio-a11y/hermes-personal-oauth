Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal Google integration named Italo Hermes Agent, operated by the owner of m.marchiorello@gmail.com. It is a personal-use assistant, not a service offered to the general public.

Google information accessed

Depending on the task and authorization, the integration can access email content and metadata, calendar information, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, contacts and tasks. It requests Gmail read, send and modify permissions and the corresponding access permissions for Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets, Docs, Slides and Tasks.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the owner’s assistant features, including retrieval, summaries, preparation of documents and messages, and authorized productivity actions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this integration to train general-purpose AI models.

AI providers and other processing

Relevant information may be sent to the AI provider configured in Hermes to interpret requests and generate responses. Provider processing, retention and any provider-specific settings are governed by that provider’s terms and the owner’s configuration. Approved Google-service actions send the required information back to Google. This is not an offline-only integration.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner’s Hermes environment. Conversation history, task outputs and derived notes may be retained in the owner’s configured local or synchronized systems until the owner removes them. Original Google data remains in Google unless an authorized action changes it. Credentials and private Google content are not included in this public website or its source repository.

Sharing and human access

The integration does not offer third parties access to the owner’s Google data. Information is processed through the owner’s configured services as described above. The owner can access retained conversations and outputs and can explicitly authorize particular sharing actions.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke the integration in Google Account connections, remove locally stored credentials, and delete retained conversations or derived outputs from the systems where they were stored. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not itself delete previously retained copies.

Google API data policy

The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not request Google authorization, collect Google account data, run analytics scripts, or set application cookies. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information under its privacy statement.
